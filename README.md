### ✦ The Concept

**Memory House** is not a standard gallery—it is a temporal experience. Instead of scrolling through a massive grid of unrelated photos, the portfolio is divided into specific times of the day, reflecting how light and mood shift as hours pass. 

Each "room" in the house corresponds to a specific hour, featuring expandable mood-to-mood navigation, discovery mechanics, and parallax architectural elements to create a fully immersive viewing environment.

### ✦ The Architecture (Time & Space)

The project is structurally mapped to the solar cycle. Visitors navigate through distinct HTML environments, each tuned to a specific atmosphere:

*   🌅 **[ Dawn ]** — *The awakening. Cool tones and quiet streets.*
*   ☕ **[ Morning ]** — *Harsh shadows and structural contrast.*
*   ☀️ **[ Afternoon ]** — *Peak exposure and vibrant life.*
*   🌇 **[ Dusk ]** — *The golden hour and falling shadows.*
*   🌆 **[ Evening ]** — *Artificial lights and twilight transitions.*
*   🌃 **[ Night ]** — *Long exposures and urban neon.*
